# Release 12.19.2025

## Weekly Job Picks

We’re adding **Weekly Job Picks (WJP)**, a curated set of top job listings published each week and grouped by profession. This is available both on the **Landing Page** and the **Professional App.**

**What’s New**

* **Landing Page WJP**
  * New Weekly Job Picks page:
    * Automatically shows the latest weekly collection (3 preview cards per category)
    * Jobs are grouped into different categories, such as **Nurse, Medical Doctor, Radtech, and Allied Health**
  * Category Specific pages:
    * These pages show only the curated jobs for that profession and week (Minimum of 6 jobs and maximum of 12 jobs per category)
    * Sorting and filtering controls are hidden to preserve the curated order.

## Recruiter Notes

We’ve enhanced **Recruiter Notes** to allow companies to optionally share notes directly with job applicants. This builds on the existing internal notes system while introducing clearer visibility controls, safer defaults, and confirmation steps to prevent accidental sharing of internal information.

Notes can now be created from both the **Applicant Profile** and **Specific Job Pages**, with consistent behavior across entry points.

**What’s New**

* **For Providers**
  * We’ve added clearer visibility options when creating notes:
    * Team only (default)
    * Applicant
    * Team + Applicant
  * **From Applicants Page**
    * A dedicated Notes section shows all recruiter notes.
    * Each note displays its associated job.
    * Recruiters can add a note and select the related job.
  * **From a Specific Job Page**
    * Job association is applied automatically and cannot be edited.
    * Job titles are hidden in the Applicant Profile view to avoid redundancy.
  * New notes always appear at the top, and the Notes section is hidden if no notes exist.
  * Assessments remain available only for notes that are not shared with applicants.
