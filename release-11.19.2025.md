# Release 11.19.2025

## Streamlined Documents Upload

We’ve completely reworked the document upload workflow to better guide users through the requirements needed to complete their profile. The new guided step-by-step process clearly shows which requirements unlock specific features, helping users progress with confidence.

**What’s New**

* **Guided Upload Flow**: A redesigned step-by-step process that walks users through each requirement, from basic information to full verification.
* **Minimum requirements:** Users must complete essential fields to apply for jobs
  * Profile picture
    * &#x20;Name (First Name, Last Name)
    * &#x20;Email
    * &#x20;Mobile number
    * &#x20;Sex
    * &#x20;Birthdate
    * &#x20;Address
    * &#x20;Profession
* **Full requirements:** Once minimum requirements are met, users are guided to complete full verification by providing
  * Profession (with supporting documents)
  * Government-issued ID
  * TIN
  * Preferred payout method

## New Document Types: Board Rating Certificate & Certificate of Employment

We’ve expanded the list of supported documents to help users provide more complete and verifiable professional information. These new document types strengthen profile credibility and improve how companies assess qualifications.<br>

**What’s New**

* **Board Rating Certificate**: Users can now upload their Board Rating Certificate under the Documents tab in their profile.
* **(Provider) Job Details:** Providers can now include Board Rating Certificate as part of the required documents when creating a new job post.
* **Certificate of Employment (COE):** Users may upload one or more Certificates of Employment linked to their work experience entries.

## Optional Religious Affiliation in Profile

Religious Affiliation is now an optional field in the Personal Information section. Users may choose to disclose this information only if they wish.

**What’s New**

* Users can now optionally select a religious affiliation from a predefined list, including:
  * Roman Catholic
  * Islam
  * Iglesia ni Cristo
  * Evangelical (Protestant)
  * Seventh-day Adventist
  * Aglipayan / Philippine Independent Church
  * Jehovah’s Witnesses
  * LDS / Mormons
  * Orthodox Christian
  * Jewish
  * Buddhist
  * Other Religions / No Religion
* This field is not required during onboarding and does not affect profile completion or verification status.
* Users may update or remove this information anytime in their profile settings.![](<.gitbook/assets/unknown (22).png>)

## Student Professions

We’ve expanded our list of supported professions to include students who are currently completing their education or clinical training. This update allows students to build their profiles earlier and access opportunities suited to their level.

**What’s New**

* Added new student profession options, including:
  * Nursing Student
  * Medical Technology Student
  * Pharmacy Student
  * Psychology Student
  * Nutrition and Dietetics Student
  * Radiologic Technology Student
  * Medicine Student
  * Physical Therapy Student
  * Occupational Therapy Student
* Students are not required to upload a diploma or PRC ID.



## Bug Fixes

* Fixed landing page bug where the company website hyperlink on the View All Companies page did not redirect users to the company’s webpage.
* Fixed an issue in the Client Portal where users were unable to create 24-hour shifts (e.g., Nov 15, 7 AM to Nov 16, 7 AM). The system now properly supports creating full 24-hour shift durations.
* Fixed an issue where the Locations filter in the dashboard and shifts list page appeared empty with no selectable options. The filter now correctly loads and displays all available locations.
