# Release 04.15.2026

## Timekeeping & Schedule Monitoring (FTE)

We’ve introduced a centralized Timekeeping and Schedule Monitoring system for full-time employees (FTEs), starting with selected clinic sites.

This replaces fragmented tools and provides a single source of truth for schedules, attendance, and time logs.

#### What’s New

**Centralized Schedule Upload (CSV)**

* Admins can upload structured schedules in bulk via CSV
* System validates data before generating shifts

**Unified Attendance Dashboard**

* Monitor all shifts across sites with clear statuses (Critical, Urgent, On Track)
* View assigned vs. unassigned shifts in real time

**Mobile Time-in and Time-out**

* FTEs can clock in and out directly from the app with photo verification

**Attendance Status Tracking**

* Automatically tags shifts as On-time, Late, Missing, or Not Going

**Reliever Shift Handling**

* Automatically flags shifts needing coverage and supports reliever assignment

**Conflict Detection**

* Prevents overlapping schedules and duplicate shifts during upload and reassignment

**Audit Logs for All Changes**

* All edits (time logs, assignments, overrides) are tracked with timestamps and reasons

#### What This Enables:

* A single system to manage schedules and attendance
* Reduced manual tracking and overrides
* Improved visibility into staffing risks and shift coverage

#### Notes:

* Payroll computation and salary disbursement are not included in this release
* Feature is initially rolled out to MedGrocer

This update streamlines operations, improves attendance accuracy, and brings MedGrocer timekeeping fully into Moongig.
